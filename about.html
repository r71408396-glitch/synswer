About Synswer

Synswer is an answer engine. You type a question, and it replies with a clear, direct explanation written in plain language, along with the sources it checked.

What it does

Ask anything, from "How does quantum computing work?" to "Latest AI news". Synswer looks up several public sources, filters them for relevance, cross-checks them and then explains the result in its own words, instead of handing you a list of links to dig through.

Where answers come from

  • Wikipedia for background and definitions.
  • DuckDuckGo instant answers for quick summaries.
  • A live web search for time-sensitive questions such as news, prices and scores.
  • Hacker News and Stack Overflow for technology and how-to questions.
  • Scholarly papers for science questions.

The sources used are listed under every answer so you can open them and check for yourself. Synswer is not a full Google-style web search, and headlines and titles are not full articles, so please check important facts.

Ways to ask

  • Type your question in the ask box, or tap a suggestion.
  • Attach a file such as a text, CSV, Markdown or JSON file, or an image, and ask about it.
  • Take a photo on your phone and ask what it shows.
  • Speak your question where your browser supports voice input.
  • Download an answer as a file, or a table as CSV.

Accounts and memory

You do not need an account. If you create one, Synswer can save short notes from your earlier questions and use them to personalise later answers. You can clear that saved memory at any time from the answer page.

Games

Synswer also has a games corner with 18 free games, including Snake, Tetris, Brick Breaker Survival, Slice & Dice and Space Shooter, with a search box to find a game by name. Each game has its own music, sound effects and leaderboard, with weekly and all-time rankings. Sign in to put your score on the boards.

Community (Social)

The Social page is a text-only community for signed-in members. You can share posts of up to 280 characters, like or dislike posts, reply, share a link to a post, and delete your own posts and replies. Every post is checked by an automated filter before it is published, and then appears in the feed straight away. Members can report any post or reply with the flag button, and admins review reports, hide or delete posts, and ban users who break the community guidelines.

Privacy

We do not sell your data or use your questions for advertising. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
